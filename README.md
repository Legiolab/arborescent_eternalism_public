# Arborescent Eternalism

**Arborescent Eternalism (AE)** is a philosophical and mathematical research programme about time, physical possibility, and the structure of histories.

In short: **AE keeps the tenseless block-universe picture of eternalism, while adding an objective modal structure of admissible alternative histories organised from a common root.**

One complete history is realised. The other admissible histories need not be concrete parallel universes: they may be understood as objective possibilities or nomological structure.

AE investigates whether this additional structure can help account for temporal orientation, records, agency, and low-entropy structure without introducing a moving present.

## Public research repository

This repository contains only material intended for public inspection, reproduction, and citation.

- `papers/` — public preprints and manuscripts, organised by paper.
- Each paper may contain its own `calculations/` directory with the minimal code, inputs, environment and expected outputs needed to reproduce published results.
- `shared/` — only genuinely shared public material used by more than one paper.

## Scientific status

AE is a research programme in development. Mathematical results, physical assumptions, philosophical interpretations, and open questions are kept distinct.

In particular, AE does **not** currently claim to derive the Past Hypothesis, a universal cosmological measure, or non-random free will.

## Public exposition rule

For every non-trivial published result:

1. state the question and assumptions;
2. give the necessary mathematics;
3. explain the result in plain language;
4. explain why it matters;
5. state explicitly what it does not prove.
