## Proposition 1: reversal-symmetry no-go

Let (D) be the admissible data and let (O(M)=\{o_+,o_-\}) be the two global time orientations of a connected time-orientable spacetime. Suppose an automorphism (Theta) satisfies (Theta D=D) and exchanges (o_+) and (o_-). If (F) is intrinsic relative to (D) and equivariant, then (F(D)) cannot be exactly one of (o_+) or (o_-).

**Proof.** Since (Theta D=D), (F(Theta D)=F(D)). Equivariance gives (F(Theta D)=Theta F(D)). Hence (F(D)=Theta F(D)). Neither (o_+) nor (o_-) is fixed by (Theta), so no unique orientation is selected from (D) alone. (square)

The premise is intentionally restricted. An entropy gradient, asymmetric boundary condition or other reversal-breaking datum added to (D) escapes the theorem.
